# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML, CSS and JavaScript for a lightweight, easy-to-update list

## Users

The owner reviews restaurant prospects across Osaka Prefecture for website-production outreach.

## Product Purpose

Show newly researched independent food businesses by geographic area and S/A/B confidence, excluding the earlier 100-store list.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Geographic grouping is the primary way to browse.
- Each candidate needs a rank, location, concise evidence and a direct source link.
- A rank describes the match to the website-outreach criteria, not food or service quality.
- The absence of a listed website does not prove no website exists.

## Evidence on Hand

The earlier 100-store list was retrieved from the owner's prior Osaka restaurant-list chat. New candidates are sourced from Osaka shopping street pages and corroborating store, media or directory pages where available.

## Product Principles

- Put the leads in the first viewport.
- Make uncertainty visible.
- Keep each claim traceable to a source.
- Do not repeat the earlier 100 names.
